## Usage

Needs the stylesheets from `@cybr/tokens` and `@cybr/effects`. After the install, import them in `app/globals.css`:

```css
@import "tailwindcss";
@import "../styles/cybr-tokens.css";
@import "../styles/cybr-effects.css";
```

```tsx
import { CrtOverlay } from "@/components/ui/crt-overlay";

export function SignalLost() {
  return (
    <CrtOverlay className="flex min-h-80 items-center justify-center">
      <p className="font-display text-display-md text-signal">NO CARRIER</p>
    </CrtOverlay>
  );
}
```

## States

- **Running:** scanlines and vignette over the children; the screen dips in brightness twice every 4s.
- **No vignette / no flicker:** turn either off with its prop.
- **Reduced motion:** no flicker; scanlines and vignette stay.
- Unlike the other backgrounds, the scanlines and vignette sit *above* the children. Children with their own `z-index` above 10 will cover them.

## Accessibility

- The overlays are `aria-hidden` and never take pointer events, so links and buttons inside still work.
- The flicker is 0.5 dips per second, far under the WCAG 2.3.1 limit of 3 flashes per second, and only dims to 94%.
- **The flicker cannot be paused.** It stops only under `prefers-reduced-motion` or with `flicker={false}`. Beside other content for more than 5 seconds, that falls short of WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide).
- Scanlines darken the children by up to 45% on alternate lines. Use large `--signal` or `--ink` text, and avoid small `--label` text for anything people must read.
